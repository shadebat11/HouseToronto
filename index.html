How the calculators work

Estimated qualifying income uses ~22.5% of home price as required gross household income. Based on a typical 30% Gross Debt Service ratio. Mortgage payments assume 20% down, 25-year amortization, and the rate from the global slider.

What income do I need to buy in Toronto?

Detached/semi at ~$1.13M needs ~$250k qualifying income at current rates. Condo at ~$651K needs ~$149k. Adjust the rate slider to see how rates move these thresholds.

How does the mortgage stress test work?

Federally regulated lenders qualify uninsured borrowers at the higher of contract rate + 2% or 5.25%. With 5-yr fixed at ~5.0–5.5%, qualifying rate is typically 7.0–7.5%.
